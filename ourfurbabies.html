Adopt a shelter Cat or Dog

Our shelter is home to a variety of loving pets, each waiting for the chance to join a caring family. From playful puppies and curious kittens to gentle adult companions, every animal has a unique personality and story to share.

Apply Now

Here’s how to apply:

  • Submit the adoption application form
  • Attend the Zoom interview
  • Meet our shelter animals in person
  • Visit your chosen pet to confirm your choice
  • Wait for vet clearance and schedule pick up
  • Pay adoption fee of ₱500 (cat) / ₱1000 (dog)
  • Take your pet home!
