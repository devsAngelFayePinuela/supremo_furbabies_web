ABOUT US

Supremo Furbabies

Supremo Furbabies is a cat and dog rescue shelter in Malabon, founded by Jennie Torres in 2021. It began with a few rescued animals and grew during the pandemic as more strays needed help.

With compassion and dedication, Jennie and her family care for hundreds of rescued cats and dogs. Today, the shelter continues to give stray animals a second chance while promoting responsible pet ownership.
