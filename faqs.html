Adoption and Rehoming

  • What are the requirements to adopt a pet?

    Simply browse our available pets, click "Adopt Me," and fill out the application form. Our team will guide you through the next steps.

  • How can I adopt from Supremo Furbabies?

    Applicants go through a screening process to ensure that our rescued animals go to loving homes. The process includes an online interview via Zoom and at least two (2) separate shelter visits to meet and get to know your chosen pet. Apply here.

  • Can you adopt my pet?

    We do NOT adopt owned pets. We already have 2 shelters for animals rescued from cruelty and neglect that are waiting to be adopted.

  • Can my adoption get denied?

    Yes. Some reasons for denied applications include: Not being able to keep their pet indoors, incompatibility with the household, plus other circumstances that may be damaging to the health, safety, and happiness of our shelter animals.

  • I live in the province/abroad. Can I still adopt?

    Yes, we welcome applications from individuals living in the province or city. Please contact us for more information on the adoption process.

  • Can I return my adopted pet if I change my mind?

    Unfortunately, we cannot accept returns of adopted pets. A pet is a lifetime commitment. However, if you truly can’t keep your adopted pet, please don’t abandon them on the streets or with strangers. Please return them to us so we can find another home for them.

Animal Rescue

  • Can you treat a homeless victim of hit-and-run?

    Calling us does not guarantee that the animal will be rescued due to limited space at the shelter and not enough volunteers. Hit-and-run victims need immediate care, and waiting for someone else to respond may cost the animal’s life.

Donating

  • How do I donate?

    We accept cash donations via bank transfer, Gcash and Paypal. Donations in kind are also accepted and can be delivered to the shelter or ordered from our partner shops. Check out our donation channels here.

  • Can I donate my pet to you?

    Pets are not donations. A donation is meant to help a charity organization and leaving your pet with us is the opposite of helping.

Spay/Neuther (Kapon)

  • Do you offer spay/neuter services?

    No, we do not offer spay/neuter services. Please contact other local veterinary clinic for more information.

  • Are all of your pets spayed?

    Not yet, some of them have severe condition that can’t undergo in the procedure. The shelter also lacks funds that is why we are seeking for your help.

Volunteering

  • How can I volunteer for Supremo Furbabies?

    Volunteer applicants must first attend the volunteer orientation to see what assignments suit them. Please follow our Facebook or Instagram for updates on when the next volunteer orientation is scheduled, or visit this page.

  • Can I volunteer without attending the orientation?

    Yes, you can do this by participating in our Shelter Feeding, Shelter Cleaning or Project Calm programs. These are volunteer activities that you can organize with your workmates, classmates, family or friends.

  • Is there a time quota for volunteer work?

    No. Some people volunteer only once a week, once a month, or whenever their schedule allows it. Shelter volunteer work must be within operating hours. (Monday-Saturday except Wednesdays and holidays, between 8am to 5pm)

  • Can I volunteer if I’m under 18?

    Yes, but volunteers under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian. Volunteers under 15 must be accompanied by an adult at all times while volunteering at the shelter.

Get Hired

  • What are the roles you are offering?

    We only have workers as a drivers and assistants when rescuing the dogs to a different areas.

  • Should we own a vehicle in order to apply?

    Not necessarily, we often rent vans or cars to rescue the dogs and cats

  • What are the requirements to apply?
    • Resume/Bio-data
    • Valid government-issued ID
    • Barangay Clearance / Police Clearance
    • Driver's License (For driver role)
    • Medical Certificate
    • Vaccination Card
    • Interview and practical assessment/trial rescue shift
