PRIVACY POLICIES

We collect personal information such as your name, contact details, and housing information to evaluate adoption applications and ensure animal welfare. Your information is kept confidential and will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.


Details may be shared with licensed veterinarians or microchip registries when necessary for animal care or registration. By submitting an application, you consent to the use of your data for the adoption process, which may include background or reference checks.


We maintain industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure.
